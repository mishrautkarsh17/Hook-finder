2-Tier Growth Distribution Framework

Which Promos To Post First, Where, and Why?

TIER 1: COLD USER ACQUISITION TikTok / Reels / Shorts

Deploy Rank #8 (Ep 1) & Rank #1 (Ep 3) First

Why: Zero Lore Requirement. Cold scrollers know nothing about the characters. Episode 1 taps into the universally understood altar betrayal archetype. Visceral visual and verbal cruelty creates instant moral alignment within 2 seconds.

  • Duration Sweet-Spot: 28s to 38s (curbs drop-off before 40s mark).
  • Hook Formula: Direct personal insult / public humiliation.
  • Call to Action: Cuts as lead hardens into defiance, prompting app download.
TIER 2: PAYWALL CONVERSION In-App / Retargeting Ads

Deploy Cross-Episode Teasers (e.g. Ep 3 + Ep 4)

Why: Spliced Curiosity Gap. Users dropped off at Episode 10+ paywalls already know the universe. Multi-segment promos bridge an episode-ending climax with the next episode's escalation.

  • Segment 1 (End of Ep N): Unresolved crisis / death threat.
  • Segment 2 (Start of Ep N+1): Shocking arrival / unexpected savior.
  • Conversion Goal: Forces coin purchase to see the outcome.

Kuku Problem 1 • Engineering Transparency

Honest Quality Audit: Failure Analysis

Where Does the Tool Fail? (Documented Edge-Cases & Concrete Remedies)

FAILURE CASE #1

The "Lore / Prop" Trap

In mid-season episodes (Ep 30-45), scripts cut on characters finding a secret document or ring.

Problem: The tool scores this high due to swelling audio, but cold scrollers don't recognize the name on the document.

Remedy: Penalize cliffhanger score if closing entity was not established in the first 10 seconds.

FAILURE CASE #2

False-Positive Action Hooks

A sudden door kicked open or guard drawing steel triggers a 10/10 hook score.

Problem: If the following 15 seconds slow down into legal/exposition dialogue, audience drops off.

Remedy: Add an energy decay metric verifying dialogue pace sustains opening intensity.

FAILURE CASE #3

Script vs Performed Pacing

A dramatic pause or tense gaze is written as a 2-second action line in the script.

Problem: In live production, an actor or editor may hold the silence for 6 seconds, dragging pacing.

Remedy: Multimodal audio/visual cross-alignment during post-production.
