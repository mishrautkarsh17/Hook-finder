1. Executive Framing & Business Impact

Microdramas live and die on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts customer acquisition costs (CAC). Today, human video editors spend hours screening 50+ episodes manually to find ad clips. We built an autonomous promo detection engine that replaces manual scrubbing with a hybrid temporal-filtering funnel: it screens an entire 50-episode show in under 3 minutes, outputs broadcast-ready sub-second timestamp cut-sheets, and cuts operational screening time by 90%+.

  • Cross-Episode Continuity: Splicing an episode-ending cliffhanger with the next episode's confrontation produces high-converting retargeting ads.

2. System Architecture: The 2-Stage Hybrid Funnel

Sending entire raw screenplays to an LLM context window is slow, expensive, and unreliable for sub-second editing. We engineered a strict division of labor between deterministic code and generative AI:

Pipeline Layer What Code/Rules Handle ($0 Cost, Instant) What Generative AI Handles (Gemini 3 Flash)
1. Parser (parser.py) Normalizes time formats into float seconds; extracts speaker emotions, camera angles, sound FX. None — Deterministic regex extraction preserves 100% ground-truth temporal accuracy.
2. Candidate Generator (candidate_generator.py) Enforces strict 20.0s–60.0s window geometry; discards mid-sentence starts; suppresses overlapping redundant clips ($IoU > 12s$). None — Pure algorithmic optimization filters 4,000+ raw blocks down to the top 3–5 viable windows per episode.
3. Semantic Scorer (scorer.py) Calculates baseline heuristic energy; provides automated offline NLP fallback if API quota exhausts. Deep Qualitative Evaluation: Evaluates standalone audience clarity; generates Hook & Cliffhanger psychological conversion rationales.
4. Ranker & Studio (ranker.py, server.py) Global composite ranking: $\text{Score} = 0.40H + 0.40C + 0.20S$; serves React 18 SPA; exports Premiere cut-sheets. Swappable LLM interface: model-agnostic REST integration supports Gemini Flash, GPT-4o, or Claude Sonnet without code changes.

4. Strategic Distribution: Which Promos to Post First & Why?

A common mistake is posting promos in episode order or simply choosing the highest numerical score. We developed a Two-Tier Growth Marketing Framework based on paid user acquisition funnel stages:

Tier 1: Cold User Acquisition (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)Top of Funnel

Deploy Rank #8 (Episode 1: Altar Betrayal) and Rank #1 (Episode 3) first.

  • Zero Lore Overhead: A cold scroller knows nothing about Ashburton lore. Episode 1 taps into the universally understood abandoned bride at altar archetype. Instant moral polarity (cruel sister vs innocent bride) hooks cold users in 2 seconds.
  • Optimal Ad Retention Curve: Top performers average 28–38 seconds. On vertical feeds, ad drop-off spikes after 40 seconds; these clips resolve their primary hook and hit the cliffhanger cut before viewer drop-off.
Tier 2: Retargeting & Paywall Conversion (In-App / Mid-Funnel)High Intent

Deploy Cross-Episode Multi-Segment Promos (e.g. Rank #5: Ep 3 + Ep 4).

  • Paywall Re-engagement: Microdrama apps typically place coin paywalls at Episode 10. Users who drop off here already know the characters. Splicing an episode climax with the next episode's escalation creates an irresistible curiosity gap.
  • Escalation Payoff: Segment 1 establishes the immediate threat; Segment 2 teases the unexpected resolution, driving coin purchases and app installs.

5. Quality Audit: Where Does the Tool Fail? (Honest Failure Analysis)

Real-world AI deployment requires clear awareness of boundary conditions. Through empirical spot-checks across the 282 generated outputs, we isolated three consistent failure modes:

  • 1. The "Lore / Prop" Cliffhanger Trap (Context Independence Failure): In mid-season episodes (e.g. Ep 28–42), screenplays frequently cut on a character discovering a document, ring, or will (e.g. "The second royal decree"). The algorithm flags this as a high cliffhanger due to swelling music cues and gasps. However, for a cold social media scroller, this cut-off produces zero urgency because they do not recognize the name on the document. Remedy: Penalize cliffhanger scores when the final block refers to named entities absent in the first 10 seconds.
  • 2. False-Positive Visual Action Hooks: Sudden physical actions (e.g. a heavy door slammed open, rapid camera snap, or guard drawing steel) trigger high hook scores even when followed by 15 seconds of slow, exposition-heavy legal dialogue. Remedy: Add an energy decay metric that verifies dialogue pace sustains the opening spike through seconds 3 to 10.
  • 3. Script-to-Screen Temporal Pacing Discrepancies: Screenplays denote a dramatic silence or tense gaze as a single line (2 seconds). In live-action execution, an actor or editor may hold the silence for 6 seconds, causing the promo's momentum to lag. Remedy: Video multimodal alignment during post-production.

6. Production Prototype: React Web Studio & Interview Tool

We built a full-stack production dashboard (React 18 SPA + FastAPI) designed for daily use by social media growth teams and live interview validation:

  • Live Interview Re-Run Engine: Drop any unseen screenplay (.txt) or an entire 50-episode ZIP archive. The system dynamically extracts blocks, filters temporal slices, and evaluates promos live via Gemini API.
  • One-Click Video Editor Cut-Sheet Export: Generates standardized timecode sheets with camera angles and dialogue cues for immediate NLE ingestion (Premiere/CapCut).
  • Universal Campaign Filters: Filter by Stop-Scroll Hooks (≥9), Severe Cliffhangers (≥9), Cold-Audience Friendly, and platform duration brackets (<30s, 30–45s).
